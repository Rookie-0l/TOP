Description --

Sushi is a traditional Japanese dish made with seasoned rice, often paired with fresh fish, seafood, or vegetables.

Known for its delicate flavors and beautiful presentation, sushi is a favorite among people who enjoy light and refreshing meals.

Ingredients :

  • Sushi rice
  • Fresh salmon
  • Nori (seaweed)

Steps for making :

  • Cook and season the sushi rice.
  • Place rice and salmon on nori.
  • Roll tightly, slice, and serve.
