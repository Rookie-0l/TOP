Description --

Butter chicken is a beloved Indian dish featuring tender chicken cooked in a creamy tomato-based sauce with butter and aromatic spices.

Rich, smooth, and full of flavor, it pairs perfectly with warm naan bread or steamed rice.

Ingredients :

  • Chicken
  • Butter
  • Tomatoes

Steps for making :

  • Marinate the chicken with spices and yogurt.
  • Cook the chicken until browned.
  • Simmer in a creamy tomato and butter sauce.
