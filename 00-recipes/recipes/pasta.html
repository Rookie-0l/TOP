Description --

Pasta is a classic Italian dish made with various types of noodles combined with sauces, vegetables, cheese, or meat.

From creamy Alfredo to tangy tomato sauce, pasta offers endless delicious combinations for every taste.

Ingredients :

  • Pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps for making :

  • Boil the pasta until tender.
  • Heat the tomato sauce in a pan.
  • Mix the pasta with sauce and add Parmesan.
